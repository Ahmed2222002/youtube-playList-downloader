About Free Playlist Downloader

Free Playlist Downloader is a simple, fast, and free desktop application to download YouTube videos or entire playlists with customizable format and quality.

Features

  • Download single videos , full playlists or subset of playlist
  • Select desired quality and format (MP4, MP3, etc.)

How to Use

  1. Paste the YouTube video or playlist URL in the input field
  2. Select the output quality and format
  3. Choose the folder where you want to save the files
  4. Click Fetch, then click Start
